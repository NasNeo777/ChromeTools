隐私权政策 · Privacy Policy

**适用扩展：AI工具箱（原 DeepSeek 工具箱）**
**更新日期：2026-11-02 · 版本 1.6.0**

中文版

AI工具箱提供视频和网页的总结、翻译、分析、练习与批改。本扩展不含广告、统计或跟踪代码，开发者不运营接收用户内容的中转服务器。

读取与发送的数据

仅在您主动点击功能或提交作答时，扩展读取相关内容：视频字幕；在评论印证模式下读取的热门评论；普通网页已经加载且可读取的正文；用于追问的对话；用于AI批改的题目、参考答案、评分要点与填空/问答作答。页面标题、网址等来源信息可能随相关请求发送。选择题本地判分，未作答题目本地记零。

网页提取会避开表单输入、可编辑区域、隐藏内容和导航等非正文区域，不读取密码输入值、跨域iframe、未加载的链接内容或视频画面。扩展不会因安装或浏览页面而自动发送正文。页面中本来公开显示的个人信息可能属于正文，请按需使用功能。

服务商与自定义地址

AI请求直接从浏览器后台发送到您选择并配置的服务商：DeepSeek、Kimi、MiMo、OpenAI、Claude，或您自行设置的OpenAI兼容API地址。当前请求只使用当前服务商的Key，不将其他服务商Key发送给它。使用自定义API地址时，您的Key与请求资料会发送至该地址。

数据处理与保留受所选服务商自己的政策约束，请在其官方网站查阅：[DeepSeek](https://www.deepseek.com/)、[Kimi](https://www.kimi.com/)、[MiMo](https://platform.xiaomimimo.com/)、[OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)、[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)。开发者不代替服务商承诺其保留期限。

本地配置与保留

各家API Key、模型ID、API地址、所选服务商、总结风格与输出语言保存在 `chrome.storage.sync`。若浏览器启用同步，Google可能在您的设备之间同步这些配置。旧版DeepSeek配置继续保留兼容。视频/页面资料、试卷与作答只保存在当前页面内存，刷新或离开后清除；主动导出的文件保存到浏览器下载位置，由用户管理。卸载扩展会删除扩展配置，不会删除已导出的文件。

权限与安全

为在普通网页显示入口和读取正文，扩展在HTTP/HTTPS页面运行；浏览器内部或其他受限页面不支持。远程AI请求使用HTTPS，API Key只发送到所配置的目标地址，且不随跨域重定向转发。我们不出售数据，不建立广告画像，不读取浏览历史。请勿在不可信的共享设备上保存私人Key。

变更与联系

后续功能变化会相应更新本政策。如需反馈，请使用本项目的公开反馈渠道。历史1.5版本政策保存在项目的 `docs/legacy/PRIVACY-1.5.md`。

English

AI Toolbox (formerly DeepSeek Toolbox) summarizes and analyzes videos and web pages and provides quizzes, grading, follow-up chat and exports. It contains no advertising or analytics code and uses no developer-operated relay server.

Content is read and transmitted only when you trigger a feature or submit answers. Depending on the feature, requests may include video transcripts, popular comments, rendered page text, source metadata, chat history, questions, reference answers, rubrics and subjective student answers. Multiple-choice and blank-submission scores are computed locally. Form inputs, editable areas and hidden page content are excluded from page extraction. The extension does not read browser history, unloaded links, cross-origin frames or video pixels.

Requests go directly to your selected provider (DeepSeek, Kimi, MiMo, OpenAI, Claude) or your configured compatible API endpoint. Only that provider's key is used. Custom endpoints receive the credentials and content you submit to them. Provider data handling is governed by that provider's own policy.

Keys and preferences are stored in `chrome.storage.sync` and may be synchronized across your devices by the browser. Existing DeepSeek settings remain compatible. Source text, quizzes and answers remain in page memory until reload or navigation; exported files remain in your downloads until you delete them. Uninstalling removes extension settings but not exported files.

HTTP/HTTPS site access enables the launcher and requested text extraction on ordinary pages. AI requests use HTTPS and do not follow redirects. The developer does not sell data or use it for profiling. This policy was updated on 2026-11-02 for version 1.6.0.
